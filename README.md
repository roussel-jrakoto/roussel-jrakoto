## 🚀 Featured Project — Tranonk'Art

<div align="center">

[![Tranonk'Art](https://img.shields.io/badge/🎨_Tranonk'Art-Cultural_Art_Platform-6366F1?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/roussel-jrakoto)

</div>

> **Tranonk'Art** is a web platform empowering artists to publish and share their artworks, while giving collectors a space to discover talent, follow creators, and support them through secure donations — bridging culture, creativity, and technology.

**Key highlights:**
- 🎨 Artist profiles with artwork publishing & management
- 🔍 Discovery engine — browse, like, and follow artists
- 💳 Secure donation system to support creators directly
- 🌍 Built to scale — designed for the African creative ecosystem

**Stack used:** `TypeScript` · `React` · `NestJS` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prisma`
